South Carolina Pride Movement is a non-profit organization based in Columbia, SC, that aims to bring together the state’s LGBTQ+ community and its allies with events centered around visibility, unity and advocacy.

SC Pride’s Rock Out! theme was inspired by the glam rock artists of the 1970’s.
I developed the visual language throughout the festival season, including the 2026 Rock Out! logo, 40+. social media graphics, print flyers and posters for three major events, and large format festival signage with attendance of 10,000+ visitors.
I designed all three billboard graphics for SC Pride’s major events, reaching titular cities throughout the Carolina’s and Augusta, Georgia.
